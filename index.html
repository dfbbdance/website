About DFBB

We focus on dance covers and group performances, with projects spanning K-pop, C-pop, and beyond. We value the effort that goes into each project and the friendships that grow along the way.

Our mission

To share our love of dance and East Asian pop culture through rehearsals, dance covers, filming, and performances, and to bring people with shared interests together in Boston.

关于 DFBB

我们以舞蹈翻跳和齐舞演出为主，作品涵盖 K-pop、C-pop、宅舞及其他喜欢的编舞。我们珍惜每个作品中的共同投入，也珍惜在排练、拍摄和演出中逐渐建立的友谊。

我们的使命

通过共同排练、翻跳、拍摄与演出，分享对舞蹈及东亚流行文化的热爱，让兴趣相近的人在波士顿相识、一起投入作品。

DFBB Dance Crew is operated by DFBB Dance Inc., a nonprofit organization recognized by the IRS under section 501(c)(3). Recognition was approved October 22, 2026, effective September 30, 2026.
